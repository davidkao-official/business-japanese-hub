Marks — the only ornaments

Every mark comes from how Japanese work documents and study material are actually marked up. A mark is never the sole carrier of meaning.

Marker 蛍光

共有が難しい見込みです。

This Japanese matters. Paired with a gloss numeral or an accessible name.

Gloss numeral 丸数字

担当部署②を明確にする。

Links a phrase to its explanation. Real characters, so kinsoku still applies.

Never

  • Uppercase mono English eyebrows (BUSINESS / CONTEXT)
  • “01 / 02 / 03” numbered feature cards
  • Floating hero cards, glass, gradients, glow
  • Torii, sakura, hanko, wave, indigo-dye or vermilion “Japan” motifs
  • Japanese fonts for Chinese text, or Chinese fonts for Japanese
  • Progress rings, mastery %, streaks, XP, invented stats
  • English labels inside a Chinese or Japanese interface
  • Internal terms in UI copy (evidence, runtime, Workplace Learn)
