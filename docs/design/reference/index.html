Source & Gloss

Every learning unit has the same shape: a piece of Japanese source material (what you must be able to use at work), a Japanese commentary (解説: why it works, what to watch), and your own record (attempts, mistakes, saves). V1 is written entirely in Japanese for people who have passed JLPT N1. The system's job is to keep those three layers distinct on every screen when all three are in the same language.

Three roles, one language

In V1 everything is Japanese, so language cannot separate the layers. Role does: typeface, surface and position. This follows the convention of Japanese textbooks and business books, which set 本文 or 例文 in Mincho and 解説 in Gothic.

Post-V1 slot: support language (not shipped in V1)

A later, opt-in layer for learners who want a note in their own language. It sits below the Japanese 解説 and never replaces or interleaves with it. It is set in that language's own face (zh-Hant, zh-Hans, ko, en tokens are reserved) and keyed by locale in the content data. V1 designs, copy and QA do not depend on it.

Marks: the only ornaments

Every mark comes from how Japanese work documents and study material are actually marked up. A mark never carries meaning on its own.

Marker 蛍光ペン

共有が難しい見込みです。

This Japanese matters. Paired with a numeral or an accessible name.

Numeral 丸数字

担当部署②を明確にする。

Links a phrase to its 解説. Real characters, so kinsoku still applies.

ログインPlus について

Never

  • Uppercase mono English eyebrows (BUSINESS / CONTEXT)
  • “01 / 02 / 03” numbered feature cards
  • Floating hero cards, glass, glow, decorative gradients (the Tachiko porcelain head and family glyph are the only gradients)
  • Torii, sakura, hanko, wave, indigo-dye or vermilion “Japan” motifs
  • Copy that assumes one shared native language among learners
  • Furigana on common kanji (the audience has passed N1)
  • Progress rings, mastery %, streaks, XP, invented stats
  • 〇×, 正解／不正解 or ok／ng colours on Career Game outcomes
  • The marker on navigation, focus, selection or Plus (it marks Japanese only)
  • 〇× or answer colours for system save / access states (use the state notice)
  • English labels or internal terms (evidence, runtime) in the Japanese UI
